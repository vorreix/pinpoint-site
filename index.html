You always think of the right words on the way home. Pinpoint gives you that moment in advance: one real situation a day, four ways to answer, and the one that actually lands — with the reasoning, so it sticks.

Real situations

A manager, a landlord, a friend who owes you money. 1,501 of them across ten areas of life.

Yours, privately

No account needed. Your progress stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up.

How it works

Each day you get one situation written the way it would actually reach you — a message, a request, a comment across a table. You pick the response you would give. Then Pinpoint shows you the sharper version: clear, calm and brief, with three short reasons it works and a line worth remembering.

Over time it builds five things: boundaries, clarity, brevity, composure, and taking criticism without flinching. The app tracks which you are strongest at, based on the answers you actually chose.
