Finishing your sign-in

If Pinpoint is installed on this device, it should have opened already and signed you in. If you are reading this, the link opened in a browser instead.

What to do

  • Open the link on the device where Pinpoint is installed. Sign-in links only work on the device that asked for them, which is what stops someone else using a forwarded email to get into your account.
  • Don't have the app yet? Install Pinpoint first, request a new link from the sign-in screen, then tap it on that device.
  • Link expired? They are single-use and time-limited. Just request a new one.

You don't need an account

Pinpoint works fully without signing in. An account only backs up your streak and history so they survive a lost phone and follow you to another device. You can carry on as a guest and sign in whenever you like — everything you have done so far comes with you.
